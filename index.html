About me

I am a research graduate student at Munich University of Applied Sciences (HM) under the supervision of Prof. Matthias Güdemann.

I also work as a research assistant at Fraunhofer AISEC, working on memory safety in CompCert. I obtained my B.Sc. in computer science at HM in 2025.

My research interests include category theory and formalization of mathematics.
